Purpose and authorised access

VAE Rent supports property owners and their authorised staff in keeping rental records, recording payments, issuing receipts and managing rental communications. Access is provided by the operator of an installation. Use only the account and property access assigned to you, and keep your credentials private.

Accurate records and communications

Operators and staff are responsible for checking the information they enter, reconciling recorded payments with money actually received, reviewing corrections and supplying accurate recipient details. A record or reminder does not itself change a rental agreement or establish that a payment was received. Rent changes and tenancy decisions must be handled through the appropriate agreement and operator procedures.

Connected services

Only connect Google accounts you are authorised to use. Gmail and Drive features depend on the permissions granted, the operator’s configuration, service availability and provider limits. Delivery history should be reviewed when a message fails or its outcome is uncertain. The privacy policy explains the data involved.

Operator responsibilities

The operator manages staff access, server availability, retention, backup storage and recovery arrangements. Keep the backup recovery password protected and separately recoverable. Before relying on reports or reminders, verify opening balances, contacts, issuer details and configuration for the actual properties.

Support

Contact itzkhartel@gmail.com about the application or its Google connections. Tenants should direct questions about their rent, payments or agreement to their property manager.
