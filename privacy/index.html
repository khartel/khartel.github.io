Data and permissions

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how VAE Rent handles information for rental-management workflows and its optional Google connections. The property owner operating an installation manages its records, staff access and operational retention. The application contact is itzkhartel@gmail.com.

Information used for rental management

An operator and authorised staff may enter staff accounts, tenant names and contact details, property and unit information, agreements, rent schedules, opening balances, recorded payments and correction reasons. VAE Rent stores receipt documents, communication content, delivery attempts and activity history to support those workflows.

Payment records describe payments entered by staff. The current application does not collect card details or process online rent payments.

Google account information and Gmail

When an authorised administrator connects Gmail, Google provides an account identifier and verified email address so VAE Rent can identify the selected sender. The connection requests openid, email and gmail.send.

VAE Rent uses the connected sender to transmit receipts, replacement or void notices, rent reminders, property-related messages and operator-requested test emails. Google receives the destination address, message content and any receipt PDF included in a send. Copies may be retained by Gmail and recipients according to their own settings.

The connection does not request permission to read or search the Gmail inbox. Stored Google authorisation tokens allow the enabled sending workflows to run without repeatedly asking the administrator to sign in. Tokens for the application sender are encrypted in its database; the operator protects the separate encryption key.

Google Drive recovery backups

An operator may separately connect Google Drive for recovery backups using drive.file. This permits the backup connection to manage files and folders it creates, rather than giving it general access to every file in the account.

The backup process creates encrypted recovery snapshots containing rental records, receipt documents and delivery/activity history, together with private application configuration and the matching application source. Private configuration can contain database credentials, Google client credentials and the key needed to recover the application’s stored sender authorisation. These contents are encrypted before transfer to the operator-selected Drive account. The recovery password is kept separately from that Drive repository.

Authorisation credentials for the Drive backup tool are stored in owner-only configuration on the operator’s computer. Backup verification may download and decrypt a snapshot privately to check that recovery works. The operator’s configured retention governs when older snapshots are removed.

Purpose and limits on Google data use

Google account information and authorisation are used to provide the connected communication and recovery features described here. VAE Rent does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.

VAE Rent’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. People may access operational data only where needed for the authorised rental workflow, user-approved support, security or applicable legal obligations.

Access, sharing and security

Property owners control authorised staff access. Managers can access records within their assigned properties; Super Admins have broader administrative access. The operator may export permitted records and issue documents or messages to the intended recipients. Operational information is also processed by the operator’s selected hosting and Google services where those features are used.

VAE Rent uses individual staff accounts, access checks and activity records. Operators are responsible for protecting their server, account credentials, backup recovery password and configured network access. The public information website has no login form, advertising, analytics script or application-data connection. Its hosting provider may process ordinary website request information under its own policies.

Retention, correction and deletion requests

Rental, financial and audit records may need to be retained by the property operator. Archiving a tenant or agreement preserves its history and is not deletion. Backup copies may remain until their retention cycle removes them. Contact the property operator for access, correction or deletion requests relating to a tenancy; the operator will assess the request and any applicable record-retention obligations.

For Google connection questions or requests concerning application-controlled Google account information, contact itzkhartel@gmail.com. Removing an authorisation does not retract emails already sent or automatically erase retained tenancy or backup records.

Disconnecting Google access

An authorised Super Admin can disconnect the Gmail sender through VAE Rent’s Email settings. The operator can stop Drive backup jobs and remove the backup authorisation. A Google account holder can also revoke an app through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorised access; it does not automatically delete existing Drive backups or received emails.

Changes and contact

Updates to these data practices will be reflected here. Material changes to Google data use require updated disclosures and any necessary consent. Questions can be sent to itzkhartel@gmail.com.
