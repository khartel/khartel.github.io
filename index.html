Rental management for property owners

Clear records.
Thoughtful follow-up.

Keep properties, rental agreements and recorded payments in one place. Give authorised staff the tools to issue receipts and follow up rent across the properties they manage.

VAE Rent is operated through owner-managed installations. Staff access is provided by the property owner or their designated administrator.

From a payment to a clear record

01 · Record

Know what was received

Record payments against rental agreements, review outstanding rent and retain a history of corrections.

02 · Receipt

Keep the evidence

Generate PDF receipts for download or email, with retained records of issuance, replacements and voids.

03 · Follow up

Stay in touch

Send property-related messages and rent reminders through an authorised Gmail connection, with delivery history for staff to review.

Google connections with a specific purpose

The operator may connect Gmail to send rental communications and Google Drive to store encrypted recovery backups. Each connection is authorised separately. The privacy policy explains the information used, the permissions requested and how access can be revoked.
